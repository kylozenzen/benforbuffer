What I’d build with you.

A fast, high-level blueprint so you can see how I think about Discord, Reddit, and community-led growth at Buffer.

Discord · The living room

  • Warm, human onboarding that tags creators vs. small teams.
  • Weekly rituals (wins, workflows, open studio sessions).
  • Creator Circles: small cohorts for deeper support.
  • Private lanes for power users and structured feedback.

Reddit · Honest workflows

  • Subreddit focused on “show your system,” not self-promo.
  • Weekly threads for workflows, hurdles, and office hours.
  • Transparent feature conversations where appropriate.
  • Moderation that keeps it useful, not corporate.

Social · Comment-first presence

  • Daily passes through LinkedIn, Threads, X, Bluesky.
  • Replies that spotlight community wins and experiments.
  • Turn high-signal threads into case studies and resources.
  • Stay tightly aligned with Advocacy and Growth on tone + timing.

Signals · Power users & insights

  • Identify power users across Discord, Community, and social.
  • Set up a light beta/advocate group with clear perks.
  • Ship a simple “community signals” digest regularly.
  • Share honest wins, frictions, and learnings with the wider team.

Days 0–30 · Listen & map

  • Audit Discord, Reddit mentions, Community usage, and prior programs.
  • Meet with Marketing, Advocacy, Product, and Engineering.
  • Host light listening sessions with community members.
  • Start a “Community Signals” doc to capture patterns.

Days 31–60 · Pilot & tune

  • Launch 1–2 Discord rituals and refine based on sentiment + participation.
  • Soft-launch the subreddit with clear posting patterns.
  • Invite 10–20 power users into a defined pilot feedback group.
  • Feed insights into product and content decisions.

Days 61–90 · Scale & share

  • Double down on successful rituals and retire the rest.
  • Formalize the power user / beta program and recognition.
  • Integrate community stories into launch moments.
  • Publish a “state of the community” snapshot with honest learnings.
