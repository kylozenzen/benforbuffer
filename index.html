Platform Plans

Five platforms.
One operating philosophy.

Select a platform to see the exact operating model — what we do, the guardrails, cadence, and what success looks like.

Reddit

Help-first credibility. Humans first, brand a distant second — always.

What we do

  • Weekly "what we learned" threads: data, reflection, a real question
  • Quarterly AMAs focused on learnings, not product promotion
  • Public recap posts after every AMA: top insights + what changes

Guardrails

  • No link dumping — value lives in the post itself
  • No forced branding — helpful and humble only
  • Trust earned with clarity and receipts, not growth hacks

Cadence

  • Weekly: 1 learning thread + 3–5 quality comments
  • Monthly: 1 community question round-up
  • Quarterly: AMA + public recap

Success signals

  • People replying with their own learnings
  • Organic "Buffer mentioned" moments in other threads
  • Questions shifting from "what is Buffer?" to "how do I use it for X?"

Discord Growth Lab

A working room where people actually improve. Not a ghost town, not a firehose.

Channel structure

  • #wins-and-weird-flexes — celebrate progress, big and small
  • #feedback-swap — guided prompt, kind by design
  • #experiments — hypothesis → test → results
  • #office-hours — Buffer team rotation

Rituals (what keeps it alive)

  • Weekly feedback swap — 45 min, prompt-based
  • Monthly growth sprint — small mission, public wins
  • Bi-weekly office hours + "what we're changing next"

Guardrails

  • No "general chat as the whole server" — channels have jobs
  • No automated engagement — humans respond, always
  • Clear onboarding: first post, first win, first connection

Success signals

  • Members post wins without prompting
  • Peer-to-peer help grows — community self-sustains
  • "I'm stuck" turns into "here's what worked for me"

Social

Community becomes the content. Not marketing about community — actual outcomes.

Content pillars

  • What we learned — weekly, data-backed, real
  • Builder spotlights — recurring, workflow-focused
  • Community recaps — monthly, transparent
  • Behind-the-scenes building — as it happens

Platform intent

  • LinkedIn — thoughtful learnings + creator stories
  • X — fast insights + experiments + real quotes
  • Instagram — visual education + spotlight clips

Guardrails

  • Only community outcomes — never "marketing about community"
  • Spotlights include the workflow, not just the praise
  • Clear, human writing — minimal jargon throughout

Success signals

  • Spotlight shares from users — advocacy in motion
  • Comments become conversations, not applause
  • Creators tagging Buffer in their own wins, unprompted

Events

Depth and relationships. Workshops, teardowns, and a Community Week people actually look forward to.

Event formats

  • Monthly workshop — 10-min talk → breakouts → shareback
  • Creator teardown night — structured, kind, with receipts
  • Office hours — Q&A with visible follow-through published after

Quarterly: Community Week

  • Workshops + creator panels
  • Experiment lab sessions
  • Roadmap preview + live feedback
  • Community recognition — gratitude as strategy

Guardrails

  • Always publish recaps — learnings + what changes next
  • Breakouts over long lectures — do the work design
  • Remote-friendly timing and full accessibility

Success signals

  • Attendees post takeaways publicly after events
  • Event-to-community conversion improves over time
  • Member-led sessions emerge organically

Partnerships

Co-create value with adjacent communities. Distribution without being salesy.

Partnership formats

  • Co-hosted workshops and panels
  • Shared research drops — data that helps both communities
  • Community exchange weeks
  • Co-created resource packs (content, not templates)

Partner targets

  • Creator education communities
  • Newsletter + content platforms
  • Indie builder ecosystems
  • Agency communities and collectives

Guardrails

  • Co-create, never co-sell
  • Clear value for members — not just brand exposure
  • Recaps and assets published after every event

Success signals

  • Partner members become active contributors
  • Joint content saved/shared more than it's announced
  • Partnerships become recurring, not one-offs
90-Day Plan

Clear commitments.
Clear outcomes.

Three phases — specific deliverables at each stage and the behavioral signals that tell us what's actually working.

Days 0–30

Foundation

  • Discord Growth Lab structure + onboarding
  • Weekly Reddit rhythm established
  • First spotlight pipeline built
  • Moderation norms defined
Days 31–60

Activation

  • Weekly feedback ritual launches
  • First workshop + recap published
  • Spotlights become recurring
  • First partnership event scoped
Days 61–90

Amplification

  • Quarterly AMA + public recap
  • Community Week plan drafted
  • Monthly recap cadence locked
  • Member-led contributions growing
🟢 Trust signals
  • Repeat commenters and event attendees
  • People asking better questions over time
  • Organic Buffer mentions in peer conversations
🔵 Belonging signals
  • Peer-to-peer help increases — not just Buffer-led
  • Members sharing wins without prompting
  • Feedback threads become a weekly habit
🟠 Advocacy signals
  • Spotlight shares and unprompted "thank you" posts
  • Creators tagging Buffer in progress updates
  • New members joining because of a user story
🟡 Early warning signs
  • Discord becomes "general chat" only
  • Events feel lecture-heavy with no outcomes
  • Posts announce community instead of showing it
✓ AI helps here

Where AI accelerates

Speed and synthesis, without losing the human voice.

  • Summarize trends from community discussions
  • Extract themes from feedback for monthly recaps
  • Draft outlines — human always edits final copy
  • Turn raw notes into clear, shareable artifacts
✗ Never goes here

Humans must show up

No fake relationship-building. No canned empathy.

  • No automated replies in community spaces
  • No bot-written "personalized" DMs
  • No replacing real moderation and presence
  • No pretending AI wrote something heartfelt
