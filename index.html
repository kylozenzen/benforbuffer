If I joined Buffer…

How I’d approach community for the next chapter.

One north star, a few focused bets, and clear feedback loops.

Community north star

Buffer customers should feel:

  • Seen — their use cases actually show up in the content.
  • Supported — they know where to go when things break or stall.
  • Involved — they can clearly see “you said / we did” moments.

Discord & Reddit

One “studio,” one “workbench.”

  • Discord as the studio: rituals, cohorts, and gentle accountability.
  • Reddit as the workbench: workflows, honest questions, and search-friendly answers.
  • Clear paths for new members so “Buffer curious” becomes “I have people here.”

Signals & social

Community as a product input, not a side quest.

  • Monthly “Community Signals” digest across Discord, Reddit, and Community inbox.
  • Comment-first engagement on LinkedIn, Threads, X, and Bluesky.
  • Simple, delightful advocate / beta program that feels human, not transactional.

You’re already doing so much right: Discord as a gentle accountability space, product changes shaped by thousands of user requests, and a culture of transparency most companies would never dare to ship.

My job wouldn’t be to reinvent that — it would be to connect the dots: clearer journeys, tighter feedback loops, and more visible “you asked, we tried, here’s what happened” stories across channels.
