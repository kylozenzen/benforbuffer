How I’d approach community at Buffer.

High-level view first, then a 30 / 60 / 90-day ramp that prioritizes listening, small experiments, and clear communication.

Community North Star

Build spaces where Buffer customers feel:

  • Seen — their use-cases and constraints are reflected back.
  • Supported — they have other humans to lean on, not just docs.
  • Involved — their feedback shapes the product and story.

Discord & Reddit together

  • Discord as the “studio”—daily interaction, friendlier vibes, deeper relationships.
  • Reddit as the “workbench”—honest workflows, troubleshooting, and searchable knowledge.
  • Shared rituals (AMAs, office hours, build-in-public threads) bridged across both where it makes sense.

Social engagement layer

  • Comment-first strategy across LinkedIn, Threads, X, Bluesky.
  • Replies that highlight community stories, not just features.
  • Turn high-signal threads into blog posts, resources, or case studies with marketing.

Beta & power user programs

  • Identify advocates from Discord, Community, and social (not just follower counts).
  • Give them a lightweight, delightful program: access, context, and recognition.
  • Feed their insights back into Product, Advocacy, and Growth as a structured loop.

Days 0–30 · Listen & map

  • Audit Discord, current Community usage, Reddit mentions, and existing programs.
  • Meet with Advocacy, Marketing, Product, and Engineering to learn their priorities.
  • Host a few light listening sessions with creators / small businesses.
  • Create a simple, recurring “Community Signals” doc and start sharing early patterns.

Days 31–60 · Pilot & refine

  • Launch 1–2 small, clear Discord rituals and evaluate sentiment + participation, not just raw numbers.
  • Soft-launch a Reddit presence focused on workflows and honest questions.
  • Invite a first wave of power users into a structured pilot feedback group.
  • Report back to the team with “what we tried, what we learned, what’s next.”

Days 61–90 · Scale & systemize

  • Double down on rituals that resonate; gracefully retire what doesn’t.
  • Formalize the beta / advocate program with clear expectations and perks.
  • Integrate community stories into launch plans and ongoing content.
  • Publish an internal “state of the community” snapshot with honest wins and growth edges.
