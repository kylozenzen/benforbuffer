The community flywheel

Simple loop. Easy to measure. Hard to copy.

Loop (aka: the thing that pays rent)

  1. Value on Reddit + Social →
  2. Join Discord Growth Lab →
  3. Complete a small challenge →
  4. Share a win →
  5. Get spotlighted →
  6. Spotlight becomes new value content → repeat

Ben-coded guardrails (so we don’t get weird)

  • No fake urgency. No “10x” nonsense. (We’re adults.)
  • Help-first on Reddit. Earn attention.
  • Discord is structured. “General chat” is how communities die.
  • Spotlights include the workflow + template, not just praise.

Events cadence

Remote-friendly, repeatable, and designed to turn lurkers into contributors.

Weekly

Office hours

Rotating Buffer team + live Q&A. End with “what we’ll improve next.”

Monthly

Workshop + breakouts

10-min talk, 20-min doing, 10-min shareback. Practical & kind.

Quarterly

Community Week

Workshops, creator panels, experiments, roadmap preview, awards.

Always

Transparent recaps

Publish learnings + next actions. No big reveals. Just steady trust.

User spotlights (the real growth channel)

Gratitude as a strategy. Featuring users makes the product feel alive.

Spotlight formats

  • Creator of the Week: workflow + one result + a template
  • Built With Buffer: deeper case study library
  • Community MVP: helpfulness > follower count
  • Wall of Wins: short wins, high frequency

Consent-forward checklist

  • Opt-in form + clear usage rights
  • Let users approve the final writeup
  • Include “what they’d do differently” (reflection)
  • Link their work prominently (not buried)
